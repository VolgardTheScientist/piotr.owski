Piotr Piotrowski, Dr. sc.

Architect, engineer and researcher | Aarau, Switzerland

LinkedIn Profile

Piotr Piotrowski is an architect, engineer and researcher based in Aarau, Switzerland. He combines more than two decades of architectural practice with expertise in BIM, digitalisation and circular construction. His work connects design and project leadership with the development of practical tools that improve how buildings are planned, delivered and adapted.

His international experience spans Switzerland, the UK, Poland, France, Belgium, the US, Russia and Bahrain, from private homes to hospitals, university buildings and major transport projects. His responsibilities have included architectural team leadership and coordination with engineers and specialist consultants. He also develops digital tools and automated workflows to improve information management, coordination and decision making.

Piotr holds a doctorate from the University of Liechtenstein, where his research examined how existing BIM models can support building component reuse through digital material banks. Combining expert interviews, software development and analysis of real building data, he explored how these systems could become accessible tools for everyday practice.

He provides independent advice on BIM, digital workflows and circular construction, drawing on both project experience and research to help clients turn technical requirements into workable processes.

Disciplines

  • Architectural Services — From concept and competition design to complex buildings and infrastructure.
  • Circularity Consulting — From reuse strategies and material inventories to digital material banks.
  • BIM & VDC Consulting — From BIM strategy and coordination to automation, data workflows and quality control.
  • Research Cooperation — From applied research and industry pilots to interdisciplinary partnerships.
