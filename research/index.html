Research & Circular Construction

Research spanning digital methods for material reuse, circular construction frameworks, computational approaches to complex architectural geometry, and automated Life Cycle Assessment (LCA / EPD). ORCID: 0009-0009-4058-5684.

Digital Material Banks (DMB) & Urban Mining

Integrating Material Passports into open BIM data structures (IFC / Revit) for automated deconstruction audit, circular inventorying, and carbon valuation across Swiss building stocks.

Automated LCA & EPD Computational Pipeline

Cloud-connected parametric toolset aggregating Environmental Product Declarations to evaluate embodied carbon and circularity indicators in real-time during design synthesis.

Doctoral Research (PhD) in Advanced Architectural Geometry

Multi-criteria structural optimization, non-standard rationalization of double-curved envelopes, and direct digital fabrication workflows for complex building skins.

Digitalisation & Computational Engineering

Strategic technology consulting, complex facade geometry rationalization, parametric modeling (Rhino / Grasshopper / Python), and open BIM / IFC exchange standards for large-scale institutional developments.
