Selected Works

Residence Le Taguy (2021 · 24 m²)

Saint-Gervais Mont-Blanc, France · Private Client / CROPKA architects · Realisation (Built) — Interior Planning & Craft

Located in the Mont-Blanc region, this holiday apartment balances bespoke comfort and spatial efficiency within a compact 24 m² footprint. Surrounding alpine landscapes directly influenced the tactile material palette: natural wood veneers, local slate, bright ash wood flooring, and calm natural tones.

Karowa-Okrzei Bridge (2017 · Span: 450 m)

Warsaw, Poland · CROPKA architects | Gruner AG | Forum Architekci · Competition Entry

The Karowa-Okrzei Bridge is an important element in the complex urbanscape of Warsaw. It not only provides a necessary link in the city’s circulation but complements the panorama of the capital's historic centre. A double arch is both structural engineering and aesthetic driven. Generous headroom under the deck and retention of existing boulevard levels ensure the promenade's continuity is preserved.

Zollhaus Mixed-Use (2015 · 14,300 m²)

Zürich, Switzerland · Kalkbreite Cooperative / CROPKA architects · Competition / 2000-Watt Society Standard Target

A mixed-use urban housing proposal combining living, working, education, leisure and retail into a compact community in central Zurich, designed around the targets of the 2000-Watt Society.

Thun Crematorium (2015 · 2,485 m²)

Thun, Switzerland · Town of Thun / CROPKA | TW arch | FLOR · Open Architectural Competition

An understated and classically proportioned building form for the town of Thun crematorium. Architecture and landscape work together to create calm spaces dedicated to contemplation, with internal courtyard gardens and daylighted rooms.

TVP Broadcaster Headquarters (2015 · 23,700 m²)

Warsaw, Poland · CROPKA architects & FORUM Architekci · Competition (Honourable Mention / 4th Prize)

Competition entry awarded an honourable mention (4th prize) for Polish Television HQ, housing two large production studios and a glazed upper panoramic studio overlooking Warsaw. The newsroom forms the central hub, linked to a public plaza.

House in Łomnica (2021 (Design 2019) · 295 m²)

Łomnica, Poland · Private Client / CROPKA architects · Built — Complete Architecture & Realisation

An airy, spacious passive house in Łomnica in the Karkonosze Mountains, combining energy efficiency with charred larch (Shou-Sugi-Ban) timber cladding, anthracite ceramic roof tiles, and high-thermal-mass masonry walls.

Gillian Kit-Home (2010 · 60 m²)

Scotland, United Kingdom · Kit-Homes Collection / CROPKA architects · Concept / Kit Home System

Gillian explores traditional residential proportions adapted for prefabricated timber frame construction. The compact 60 m² system optimizes internal circulation around an open-plan living core.

Uallas Kit-Home (2010 · 170 m²)

Scotland, United Kingdom · Kit-Homes Collection / CROPKA architects · Concept / Kit Home System

Uallas is a modular residential system featuring flexible indoor-outdoor configurations developed for prefabricated timber construction and contemporary family living.

Museum of Lubomirski Princes (2018 · 7,962 m²)

Wrocław, Poland · Ossolineum National Institute / CROPKA architects · Architectural Competition

Harmoniously fitting into the historic Wrocław urban fabric with a 50-degree sloped extensive green roof dialoguing with the Ossolineum gardens. Features a perforated bronze facade inspired by an 18th-century panorama of Lviv.

Viaduc de la Baye de Clarens (2016 · Span: 130 m)

Clarens / Montreux, Switzerland · CROPKA architects | GMASP · Infrastructure Competition

An enclosed extradosed railway viaduct designed to accommodate differential geological movements over a 100-year lifetime with precast modular concrete and internal prestressed cables.

Spylaw Park (2019 · 173 m²)

Edinburgh, United Kingdom · Private Client / CROPKA architects · Realisation (Built) — Private House

An inward-focused private residence organized around a rainwater-harvesting courtyard pool. A black zinc cantilevered volume rests above a white base, capped with a planted turf roof.

Augusta Raurica (2014 · 7,253 m²)

Augst, Basel-Landschaft, Switzerland · Kanton Basel-Landschaft / CROPKA architects · Architectural Competition — Minergie-P Design Target

Archaeological finds center conceived as a flexible timber modular structure. External glu-lam frames free the internal floorplates, with Brettstapel solid timber panels and an external louvered shading envelope designed around Swiss Minergie-P energy targets.

Kindergarten Wildenstein (2014 · 424 m²)

Rorschacherberg, Switzerland · Gemeinde Rorschacherberg / CROPKA architects · Competition Entry

Educational building proposal shaped around hillside topography in Rorschacherberg. Extends across the slope under a continuous planted roof, connecting classrooms around a daylight atrium and bridging toward the primary school.

Kantonsspital Baden (2016–2023 · 76,215 m²)

Baden, Switzerland · Nickl and Partner (Lead Architect · Client: Kantonsspital Baden AG) · Architectural Project Lead (Phases 4–5)

Piotr served as Architectural Project Lead at Nickl and Partner for the new Kantonsspital Baden during project phases 4 and 5. He managed the architectural planning team, coordinated specialist consultants and represented the general planning team in discussions with the client.
